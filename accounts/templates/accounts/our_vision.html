Good things deserve a second term

Every year, HCIBS seniors move on and leave behind textbooks, uniforms, stationery and everyday essentials that the juniors need. SJ Passdown makes passing them on easy: free, and from one boarder to another.

What we believe

  • Sustainability

    Useful things shouldn't end up in the bin when a senior moves out. Passing them down keeps them in use for another year and reduces waste.

  • Free for every junior

    Nothing here is bought or sold. Items are given away, so no one misses out because of cost.

  • Seniors looking out for juniors

    A small handover is a big welcome. Every passdown is a senior helping a junior settle in.

How it works

  1. Post

    Add a few photos and a clear description of something you no longer need.

  2. Claim

    Juniors claim or browse through the listings and send a claim with a short note.

  3. Choose

    The owner looks through the claims and reserves the item for one student.

  4. Hand it over

    Chat to arrange collection, meet up, and mark the item as passed on.

Community guidelines

  • Keep it private. Don't post or share private information about yourself or other people.
  • Keep it kind. No profanities, offensive material or other inappropriate content.
  • Describe it clearly. Mention wear, missing parts or notes inside, so there are no surprises at handover.
  • Follow through. If you claimed something, collect it, or cancel your claim so someone else can have it.
